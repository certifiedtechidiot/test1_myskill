## 3. Product Catalog & Technical Specifications

### A. Interlocking Concrete Paver Blocks
Manufactured via high-pressure hydraulic vibration with a dual-layer mix (dense abrasion-resistant top layer and high-strength load-bearing core):
* **Standard**: IS 15658:2021 (Precast Concrete Blocks for Paving)
* **Compressive Strengths**: M-35 to M-50 grades
* **Profiles & Thicknesses**:
  * **60 mm (M-35)**: Residential driveways, pedestrian walkways, villa patios, light car traffic (~135 kg/m²).
  * **80 mm (M-40)**: Resort internal roads, medium commercial vehicles, coach parking, delivery bays (~180 kg/m²).
  * **100 mm (M-50)**: Heavy industrial freight corridors, container yards, port logistics (~225 kg/m²).
* **Patterns Available**:
  * Uni-decor (Geometric coastal villa patterns)
  * I-Shape Dumbbell (High-shear mechanical interlock for braking zones)
  * Zig-Zag / Tri-hex (Heavy directional traffic)
  * Grass Pavers / Porous Pavers (Rainwater infiltration & eco-parking)

### B. Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) Manhole Covers & Frames
Engineered with high tensile Fe-500D TMT rebar reinforcement and dense anti-corrosive concrete matrix:
* **Standard**: IS 12592:2002 (Precast Concrete Manhole Covers and Frames)
* **Classifications & Load Capacities**:
  * **LD 2.5 (Light Duty — 2.5 MT)**: 450 mm diameter / square; gardens, pathways, inspection chambers.
  * **MD 10 (Medium Duty — 10.0 MT)**: 600 mm square / circular; residential driveways, light commercial premises.
  * **HD 20 (Heavy Duty — 20.0 MT)**: 600 mm & 900 mm; main arterial roads, public bus routes, commercial avenues.
  * **EHD 35 (Extra Heavy Duty — 35.0 MT)**: Heavy industrial logistics facilities, cargo terminals, docklands.

### C. Cement Chequered Flooring & Parking Tiles
High friction wet-grip anti-skid surface tiles designed for coastal monsoon conditions:
* **Dimensions & Thickness**:
  * 300 mm × 300 mm × 25 mm (~60 kg/m², ~11 tiles/m²)
  * 400 mm × 400 mm × 30 mm
* **Patterns**: 16 Chequer, 24 Chequer, Wave pattern, and Tactile Blister tiles (transit accessibility / ADA compliance).
* **Applications**: Basement car parking, steep ramp aprons, terrace perimeters, transit platforms.

### D. Precast RCC Boundary Compound Walls & Post Elements
High-speed perimeter security wall system:
* **Wall Panels**: 2100 mm × 300 mm × 50 mm, tongue-and-groove joint profile, steel rebar reinforced, fire & rot resistant.
* **Column Posts**: 150 mm × 150 mm pre-slotted posts (double-slot inline and single-slot corner posts).
* **Advantages**: Fast dry-joint installation, high wind resistance, resistant to Goan saline coastal corrosion.

### E. Precast Road Kerbstones & Edging Restraints
* **Road Kerbstones**: 300 mm × 375 mm × 150 mm, bullnose bevel profile (prevents lateral paver shift and road shoulder erosion).
* **Garden Edge Curbs**: 100 mm × 200 mm × 400 mm chamfered edges for landscape boundaries.
